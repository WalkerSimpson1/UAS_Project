## Findings

### Part 108 (BVLOS)
- Not final as of early Nov 2026. Sent to OIRA on 2026-07-10 and still "Pending Review". RIN 2120-AL82.
- The EO deadline (Feb 2026) has passed. Reported statutory publication deadline: 2026-12-07 (49 U.S.C. 44811).
- Reported sticking point: right of way for Part 108 drones over manned aircraft that aren't broadcasting their position.
- Sources: [droneauthority.org](https://droneauthority.org/laws/part-108), [flyusi.org](https://www.flyusi.org/guides/get-108-ready), [sky.trade](https://sky.trade/articles/part-108-bvlos-final-rule-stuck), [flybyops.com](https://flybyops.com/blog/faa-part-108)

### FCC Covered List
- 2025-12-22: all foreign-produced drones and critical components added. This blocks new equipment authorizations; existing authorized models can still be sold.
- 2026-01-07: some categories exempted until 2027-01-01 (none are DJI products).
- 2026-06: foreign toy drones removed.
- 2026-08: proposed ban on continued import and marketing of foreign "military-grade" drones (thermal, LiDAR, docks) after a 180-day wind-down. Comments closed 2026-09-02.
- DJI v. FCC (9th Cir.): motion to dismiss denied 2026-05-28; DJI's opening brief due 2026-11-02, FCC's answering brief about a month later. Pentagon letters were added to the record in Oct 2026.
- Sources: [DroneDJ Oct 15](https://dronedj.com/2026/10/15/fcc-dji-ban-pentagon-letters/), [Iowa State CALT](https://www.calt.iastate.edu/post/foreign-made-uas-stay-fcc-covered-list-dji-and-autel-challenges-proceed), [DroneDeploy](https://www.dronedeploy.com/blog/foreign-drones-and-the-fcc-covered-list-a-fact-based-guide-for-compliance-teams), [FCC DOC-416839A1](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-416839A1.pdf)

### Section 232 drone tariffs
- Proclamation of 2026-08-13; duties effective 2026-09-03. The tiers reported (100% vs 25%, 25 kg / thermal thresholds) conflict across sources, so check the proclamation text.
- They stack on top of the Section 301 tariffs.
- Pending: Commerce market report due about mid-Dec 2026 (120 days); onshoring relief procedures not yet published.
- Sources: [Troutman Pepper Locke](https://www.troutman.com/insights/cleared-for-tariffs-what-the-new-section-232-drone-tariffs-mean-for-the-uas-industry/), [EY](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2026-1756-new-section-232-poclamation-on-drones-and-drone-components-discussed), [Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20260820/f0df3ceb427c41c2b29941d74feae12e/c.html)

### Counter-UAS
- SAFER SKIES Act enacted in the FY2026 NDAA (signed 2025-12-18). It extends C-UAS authority to state, local, tribal and territorial (SLTT) agencies.
- DHS/DOJ interim final rule (FR 2026-13609, published 2026-07-06; reported effective 2026-07-01). The FBI training center certifies; only non-kinetic mitigation is reportedly approved for SLTT agencies.
- Reported $250M FEMA C-UAS grant round. H.R. 7525 is pending, and House T&I approved a C-UAS reauthorization bill.
- Sources: [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/06/2026-13609/counter-uas-authority-for-state-local-tribal-and-territorial-law-enforcement-and-correctional), [CRS IN12661](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IN12661), [House T&I](https://transportation.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=409042), [DRONELIFE](https://dronelife.com/2026/07/07/counter-uas-rules-safer-skies-act/)

### FY2027 NDAA
- The House passed H.R. 8800 on 2026-07-22 (216–212). S. 4784 has not reached the Senate floor. Conference is ongoing in the lame-duck session; per a Nov 19 report, Rogers expects a House vote after Thanksgiving. No conference text yet.
- Drone sections: House §1736 (export license relief for drone systems), Senate §847 (drone industrial base). Their outcome in conference is unknown.
- Sources: [CRS IN12704](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IN12704), [ndaa.ai Nov 19](https://ndaa.ai/updates/2026-11-19), [Trade Compliance Resource Hub](https://www.tradecomplianceresourcehub.com/2026/11/02/congress-returns-national-security-and-trade-provisions-to-watch-in-the-fy2027-ndaa/)
